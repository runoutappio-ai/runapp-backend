The solution

One tap. One budget. One sealed envelope.

Run Out turns “where should we eat?” into a night out with a surprise built in, and turns empty tables into prepaid guests.

1

Guests set the brief

Party size, date and time, area and travel distance, budget per person (AED 50–400), vibe, cuisines to avoid, allergies.

2

They prepay

The table budget is paid up front. No-shows cost the guest, not the restaurant.

3

We match the restaurant

Our operations team picks a venue that fits every constraint. The restaurant designs the menu for that budget.

4

The envelope opens

Restaurant and menu stay sealed in the app with a countdown, and are revealed at the table time.

The supply side

Restaurants get prepaid new guests, and own the first impression.

A Run Out guest has never sat at their table. The restaurant decides the dishes, portions and price, so it decides how good that first experience will be. Done well, the guest comes back directly.

01

Pay for guests, not ads

No upfront advertising spend. A platform fee per booking, only when a prepaid guest is on the way.

02

Prepaid, no no-shows

The table budget is captured before the guest arrives.

03

No discount wars

Public prices stay unchanged. The surprise is the value, not a coupon.

04

Fills quiet slots

Off-peak tables become bookings without a public promotion.

Why Dubai, why now

A city that eats out, and keeps growing.

More choice every month means more decision fatigue for guests, and more competition for every new restaurant. Run Out sits exactly between the two.

Market size

A USD 9.6B full-service dining market in the UAE.

The UAE foodservice market is valued at USD 23.21B in 2025 and forecast to reach USD 61.21B by 2031 (17.55% CAGR). Full-service restaurants, where Run Out operates, hold 41.55% of it [8].

Our first market is Dubai, launching with a focused set of neighbourhoods before expanding across the city and the wider UAE.

People already pay an app to decide their night out.

Timeleft matches strangers for dinner and has operated in Dubai. Its growth shows that “let the app choose” is a habit people will pay for.

150,000diners joining Timeleft dinners each month (August 2025) [9]

Where we are

Guest app

iOS, Android and web
