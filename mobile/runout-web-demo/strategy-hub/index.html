Create new business for restaurants that they would not receive otherwise, help customers spend less, and let Run Out earn by creating value for both sides.

Easier for customers

Value for Run Out
Earn revenue by generating incremental restaurant business and helping customers receive better value.
Company

Business model

For now, Run Out operates exclusively through the app. A user simply enters how many people are going, where they want to go, when they want to dine and how much they want to spend. Within two hours, Run Out sends them an invitation revealing the restaurant assigned to them, together with its set menu. The customer pays the restaurant directly for the meal, while Run Out receives only the reservation fee.

Guest journey Decided
01Set the essentials

Enter the number of people, location, date and time, and budget.

02Submit and pay

Confirm the request and pay Run Out's reservation fee.

03Receive the invitation

Within two hours, receive the assigned restaurant and its set menu.

04Dine

Go to the assigned restaurant and pay the restaurant directly for the meal.

Restaurant journey Decided
01Join Run Out

The restaurant signs an agreement to become a Run Out partner.

02Create the offer

Set the menu, its price, the customer saving and the food category.

03Receive reservations

Run Out routes suitable customer reservations to the restaurant.

04Serve and get paid

Serve the guests and receive payment for the meal directly on the day.

Shisha and high-margin add-ons
Include
  • Guests can request shisha in the app in the same way they request an alcohol-serving venue.
  • Include shisha as an optional restaurant offer where the venue is legally licensed to provide it.
  • Shisha is relatively inexpensive for the restaurant compared with its selling price.
  • It can increase the guest's total bill and the restaurant's revenue.
  • Only show shisha or alcohol offers when permitted and appropriate for the venue and guest.

Customer service

Helping guests before and during their reservation.

“Did I get the reservation?”
Idea
  • Confirm that the reservation is in place.
  • Confirm the date and time.
  • Confirm the restaurant.
  • Confirm the party size.
  • Provide the reservation-fee receipt.
  • Send the details by email or WhatsApp.
Cancelling the reservation
Decided
  • The guest can cancel up to 2 hours before the reservation.
  • The date cannot be changed.
  • The time cannot be changed.
  • The restaurant cannot be changed.
  • After the 2-hour cutoff, cancellation is not available.
  • Reservation-fee handling after cancellation remains an open decision.
Lost — can't find the restaurant
Idea
  • Send the restaurant's saved map pin.
  • Send the directions provided by the restaurant.
  • Send the restaurant's parking instructions.
  • Share entrance or parking photos when available.
  • Share the restaurant's contact number.
  • Stay available until the guest finds the venue.

Lean initial budget
Low cost
Restaurant acquisition should be inexpensive because the founders will visit restaurants directly.
  • Visit restaurants in person instead of paying for a large sales campaign.
  • Group visits by area to reduce travel time and cost.
  • Use a free, open-source CRM to track contacts, follow-ups, onboarding, and status.
  • Use a digital restaurant deck instead of expensive printed materials.
  • Build the onboarding flow with existing low-cost tools first.
  • Limit spending to transport, simple materials, and test-booking costs.
  • Set the final budget after measuring the first restaurant visits.

Smart discounts to fill empty tables
Confirmed feature
  • The restaurant switches smart discounts on or off from its portal.
  • The restaurant chooses the available dates, times, and number of tables.
